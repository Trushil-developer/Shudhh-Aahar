Turn this off to immediately give every user full member-level access for free, everywhere in the app — no purchase required, no paywall shown. Billing Periods for Sale

Controls which billing periods the purchase flow offers to new buyers — e.g. launch monthly-only and turn yearly on later, no app release needed. Turning both off is not a paywall switch: use the Payments Kill Switch above for that instead. This never affects members who already bought the other period — they keep managing their existing plan as normal.
