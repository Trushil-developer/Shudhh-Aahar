Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Shuddhaahar ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Shuddhaahar Ltd. We are committed to protecting your personal data and complying with applicable data protection laws in each market where Shuddhaahar operates.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data: • Account data: name and email address (via Google Sign-In) • Health profile data: allergies, intolerances, medical conditions, medications, dietary preferences and biometric metrics (age, weight, height) — all provided voluntarily • Usage data: scan history, products scanned, diary entries • Device data: device type, OS version, app version for crash reporting • Payment data: handled entirely by Apple/Google in-app purchase — we never see or store your card details

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases: • Contract: processing necessary to provide the Shuddhaahar service you have subscribed to • Legitimate interests: improving our service, preventing fraud, security monitoring • Consent: processing special category health data (you provide this voluntarily)

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to: • Provide personalised health analysis of scanned products • Maintain your health profile and scan history • Generate aggregated, anonymised statistics for service improvement • Process subscription payments via Apple/Google in-app purchase • Send service-related communications (no marketing without explicit consent)

4. Data Storage & Security

Personal data is stored in AWS data centers. Health data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Access is controlled with owner-level permissions verified via your Google sign-in — only you can access your own health records.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to: • Access: request a copy of your personal data • Rectification: correct inaccurate data • Erasure: request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten") • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format • Restriction: limit how we process your data • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests Exercise your rights via Profile → Your Data Rights in the app, or email info@shuddhaahar.shop.

6. Data Retention

Active account data is retained while your account is active. If you cancel, data is retained for 30 days then permanently deleted. Anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained indefinitely.

7. Contact

Data Controller: Shuddhaahar Ltd Email: info@shuddhaahar.shop If you have concerns about how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
