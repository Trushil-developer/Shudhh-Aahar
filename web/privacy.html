Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Shuddh Aahar ("we", "us", "our") shops at UK supermarkets for our customers and delivers their groceries. We are committed to protecting your personal data and complying with UK data protection law.

1. Data We Collect

We collect: • Account data: your name and email address, when you sign in with Google or Apple • Delivery data: your delivery address, postcode, phone number (optional) and any delivery note • Order data: the items you order, your bills and delivery status • Shopping data: your chosen supermarket, your basket and items you save to your account • Device data: device type, operating system and app version, for fixing errors If you used our earlier health features, any health profile you saved stays private to your account until you delete it. We do not collect card details. You pay on delivery.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

• Contract: to take, shop for and deliver your orders • Legitimate interests: running and improving our service, preventing fraud and keeping it secure • Consent: for any health information you chose to give us

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to: • Check that we deliver to your postcode • Shop for, price and deliver your order • Show you your orders and bills • Keep your basket, saved items and supermarket choice in step across your devices • Contact you about your order We do not send marketing without your consent, and we never sell your data.

4. Who We Share It With

We share only what is needed to run the service: • Our hosting provider, which stores our data in the UK • A postcode lookup service, which receives your postcode to check our delivery area • Google or Apple, to sign you in Your name, address and phone number are used only to deliver your order.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in UK data centres and encrypted at rest and in transit. Only you can see your account through the app and website, and only the people who process your orders can see your order and delivery details.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

You have the right to: • Access: request a copy of your personal data • Rectification: correct inaccurate data • Erasure: request deletion of your data • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format • Restriction: limit how we process your data • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests Use Profile → Delete My Data in the app, our Delete My Data page, or email info@shuddhaahar.shop.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data within 72 hours, except order records we must keep by law.

8. Contact

Data Controller: Shuddh Aahar Email: info@shuddhaahar.shop If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
